FAQ
Questions about moving with us.

  

    
How do I get a quote for house shifting in Salem?
Call 093607 93776 or fill in the enquiry form with your two addresses, the type of move and your preferred date. We call you back with a quote before you commit.

    
Do you move offices and shops as well as homes?
Yes. We relocate offices, shops and godowns, including furniture, systems, files and stock. Tell us the size of the space and what needs to move.

    
Can I book only packing or only transport?
Yes. You can ask for packing and unpacking alone, goods transport alone, or the full pack, move and settle service.

    
Do you move to other cities?
We do intercity moves from Salem. Share your destination in the form and we confirm the details and the quote.

    
Can you move my car or bike?
Yes. Choose vehicle transport in the enquiry form and tell us the make, model and destination.

    
How early should I book?
As early as you can, especially for weekends and month-end dates. Call us to check availability for your date.
